## The class lines (the new rule, "merging must change things consistently")

`mergeResultOf` / `mythicOf` (from `@/game/data/roster`) drive everything; `cats/collection.ts` has the pure part (`lineOf`, `stepFrom`, `lineSentence`, `lineMetrics`).

- **Cats tab** (`CatsTab.ts`): a kit `SegmentTabs` class filter (all / four classes) on top, a wild-card strip (`WildStrip.ts`: one rarity-mat chip and count per rarity), then one paper page per class (`ClassSheet.ts`): the class name on a torn label in the class colour (`CLASS_ACCENT` from `@/view/hud/kit`, the same colours as the battle HUD), a round icon disc, a tape strip in the class tape colour, the role line, and under it the five cats in one row from 꼬마 to 수호신 (`LineRow.ts`).
- **The row**: five compact photo frames (`LineFrame.ts`, about 108 x 150 plus the name under it, one cell is 134 px wide on a 672 px page) joined by four die-cut arrow tags stuck over the gaps, in the class colour, labelled 합성 three times and a mustard 각성 last (English "Merge" / "Awaken"; a longer label is set at 20 px so the tag never grows over the photos). Each frame: cream border, mat in the rarity colour, rarity ornaments that pile up (dashed line, corner mounts, tape, gold star; the same ladder as the kit's `CardFrame`), level pill, card bar (`2/2`, green when ready, gold `MAX`), a green "upgrade ready" arrow sticker that pops once, and for the guardian a paper strip with the class mark instead of a bar. `CardFrame` could not be used as is: its smallest size is 150 x 200 and five of them do not fit 720 wide with arrows between them, so this is the compact frame the line needed.
- **Unit screen** (`UnitScreen.ts`, `UnitHero.ts`): a cream sheet with a teal cut line and a taped corner; the name on a torn label in the rarity colour; rank and class pills; the cat as a sticker on a two-tone paper pedestal in front of a flat paper sunburst (14 triangles, quiet tint, makes two turns in 100 seconds, no light, no glow); a round level sticker; the flavour line in a speech bubble. Below, pages with their title on a coloured label: **합성 줄** (the five portraits of the class as mini frames, this cat marked with a dashed teal ring and a piece of tape, other cats tappable, and one plain sentence: "검사냥 둘을 합치면 바이킹냥이 돼요." / "사무라이냥은 각성하면 호랑이 장군이 돼요." / for a guardian "사무라이냥을 각성하면 호랑이 장군이 돼요." / English "Two Sword Cat merge into Viking Cat."), **능력치** (dashed rows, now -> next with a leaf or berry pill for the difference), **특기**, **레벨 특전** (a checklist: paper tick boxes, unlocked rows on a pale strip), **강화 카드** (bar, own cards vs needed, wild cards, or for a guardian the note that it follows its king). The coral level-up button with its gold cost sits in the action bar with one piece of tape.
- **Level-up**: audio `upgrade` (+ stinger when a perk unlocks), haptic, the screen scrolls to the top, 70 pieces of paper confetti, a "레벨 업!" stamp slammed onto the hero (arrives big and transparent, lands with a squash and a small shake, fades after about 1.5 s), the level sticker pops and the sunburst spins up for a moment, the numbers roll (level, every stat) and the new perk's tick pops. Everything updates in place from the profile.

## The shop

`ShopTab.ts` is unchanged except through its blocks; each block is a cream page (`paperPage` in `paperBits.ts`: the kit `Panel` with a title label across the top edge and one tape strip, a different label colour per section). `signature()` / rebuild-on-change, `openShop(section)`, the spy on the segment tabs, `previewFx`, the actions and every purchase flow are as before.

- **Chests** (`blocksChests.ts`): each chest stands on a small kraft paper shelf with its owned count on a cream pill; title, odds button (all three kinds), card count and guarantee line; the gold chest shows the pity bar with the named target ("대상: 사무라이냥") and turns green with "이번 상자에 보너스 카드 8장!" at the threshold; the buy button is a `PriceTag` (luggage tag, string hole, gem icon and price and the word) or an Open button when owned; the free chest shows its countdown and the ad / gem skip.
- **Daily** (`blocksDaily.ts`): six photo cards (item on a rarity mat, count pill, name) each with a price tag (gold or gem, green "무료" tag for the free slot); bought = a 구매 완료 ink stamp and a tick on the mat. Refresh by ad with its daily limit; locked profile = a lock and the hint.
- **Gems and packs** (`blocksStore.ts`): one-time packs are horizontal die-cut coupons (stub with the chest sticker, dashed perforation with notches, a "1회 한정" bookmark ribbon), the four gem packs are vertical coupons with a value ribbon "+5%" / "+12%" / "+18%" (`gemBonusPercent`: gems per won against the cheapest pack; the cheapest has none). Passes: Butler Pass and Monthly Gem Pass as coupons (height follows the text; owned = a green "쓰는 중이에요" stamp; gem pass shows days left and the claim button); piggy bank page with a drawn sticker piggy (`piggyArt`, no art file), the pool bar always shown, the break button with its price, the free partial break.
- **Style** (`blocksStyle.ts`): rugs are the real craft mats (`buildRug(rugSkin(id))` baked once and re-parented, shown small in a paper well with their tape and dashed line), the equipped one has yellow tape and a warm card; summon effects show their own paper bits (the theme's colours) round a paw sticker, tap = the real effect. Buy = a price tag, owned = Equip, locked = a padlock and the reason.
- **Tickets**: a coupon with the ticket sticker, "n/stock" count, a price tag and the ad button.
- **Not enough**: gems / gold short = a kit dialog that says how many are missing and offers "보석 보기" (jump to the gems section) or "전투하러 가기"; unavailable stores give a toast.

## Odds screen (`OddsScreen.ts`)

Segment tabs for the three chests, then one ruled notebook page (pale teal lines cut to the table's own row pitch so every number sits between two lines, a red margin line, three punched holes, one tape strip): chest picture and title, "카드 한 장마다 나올 확률" with the kit `OddsTable` (unframed, so the page's lines are the rows), the wild-card footnote, guarantee lines, the bonus-card rule with the live counter bar and target, the "same odds whether you pay or not" note, the table version. Every number comes from `profile.oddsOf` (the test compares it with `ODDS` and `formatOdds`).

## Chest reveal (`ChestReveal.ts`, plan in `revealPlan.ts`)

The wooden floor (`drawFloor`) with the title on a paper label beside the skip button. The chest sticker drops on and lands with a dust puff, a flat shadow and a short shake, rattles with rising frequency and amplitude, then pops: a flat burst and paper confetti in the best rarity's colours. **The best-rarity tell** is a paper ribbon across the lid in that rarity's mat colour with one to four dots (`ribbonDots`), on the chest from the first frame; it never changes. Cards fly out of the chest in an arc as kraft-paper backs (dashed edge, paw print), flip one stack at a time into kit `CardFrame`s (rarity mat, ornaments), with the count rolling up on a paper pill, a "Wild" / "Bonus" tag, and a flourish table (`flourishOf`): common = dust; rare = + a tape slap; epic = + the rarity name stamped on the card; legendary = + paper confetti; the best card of an epic or legendary chest also gets a flat sunburst, a longer hold and a scale pulse. Skip (first frame, the kraft skip button, any tap on the floor, Escape) jumps to the summary: every card face up with the tape it earned (stamps are dropped so the summary stays readable), then a paper label with the totals and the OK button. The plan (about 1.5 s for a wooden chest, bounded flip phase for a huge chest) is unit-tested.

## Reward popup (`rewards.ts`)

`showRewardsPopup` is now its own `Popup` (priority 1, backdrop does not close): a cream sheet torn along its bottom edge with the title on a coral label and pink tape, a flat pale paper sunburst turning slowly behind it, and the rewards as stickers that pop on one after another with a rising chime and haptic (currencies and chests on round paper discs, cats and wild cards as small photo frames in the rarity mat), the amount counting up on a teal strip with the name under it, and one Claim button after the last sticker. Pressing Claim (or Escape / Back) sends currency sprites from each currency sticker to `shell.currencyAnchor(kind)` through `flyTo` and calls `shell.refresh()` when the last lands.

## 2026-10-07 QA fixes

An engineer began the fixes and was cut off by the quota; I finished the half-done chest reveal and went through every report (confirmed it in the browser or from the code, then fixed it at its root). What changed, by file:

- **Chest reveal** (`ChestReveal.ts`, `RevealCard.ts`, `photoPlate.ts`, `revealPlan.ts`).
